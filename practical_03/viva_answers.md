# Practical 3 - Viva Answers

**Question 1: Why use conditional statements?**

They select actions according to whether conditions are true or false.

**Question 2: How do if, if-else, and if-elif-else differ?**

They represent an optional action, two alternatives, and several ordered alternatives.

**Question 3: When are nested conditions useful?**

Use them when a second decision depends on a first decision.

**Question 4: How do logical operators help?**

and requires both conditions, or requires at least one, and not reverses a Boolean.

**Question 5: How are elif conditions evaluated?**

Python checks them in order and executes only the first matching branch.

**Question 6: Why is indentation important?**

Indentation defines which statements belong to each branch.

**Question 7: How are equal largest values handled?**

Use >= comparisons or max(), and report all matching positions when needed.

**Question 8: What is the leap-year rule?**

A year is divisible by 400, or divisible by 4 but not by 100.

**Question 9: How does a menu use conditions?**

Read a choice and dispatch to its matching if or elif branch.

**Question 10: Relational versus logical operators?**

Relational operators compare values; logical operators combine or negate conditions.
