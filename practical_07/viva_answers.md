# Practical 7 - Viva Answers

**Question 1: List versus tuple?**

A list is mutable and usually written with []; a tuple is immutable and commonly written with ().

**Question 2: Why are lists mutable?**

Their stored elements can be replaced, added, or removed after creation.

**Question 3: Why are tuples immutable?**

Their element references cannot be reassigned after creation, though a contained mutable object may still change.

**Question 4: How does negative indexing work?**

-1 selects the last item, -2 the second-last, and so on.

**Question 5: What is slicing used for?**

It extracts a sequence portion or selects values using a step.

**Question 6: Nested lists versus 2D arrays?**

Nested lists may have uneven rows and mixed types; numeric 2D NumPy arrays have a rectangular shape and common dtype.

**Question 7: What are tuple packing and unpacking?**

Packing combines values into a tuple; unpacking assigns its values to separate names.

**Question 8: Which list methods are common?**

append, insert, remove, pop, sort, and reverse are common methods.

**Question 9: When should a tuple be preferred?**

Use one for a fixed collection of values that should not be reassigned.

**Question 10: How are lists and tuples used in data processing?**

Lists hold changing records or queues; tuples hold fixed coordinates or structured values.
